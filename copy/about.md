## Reading view

> About
> **Roadmap Labs is Dan Kovac, in Melbourne.**
> Dan did ten years in digital marketing. Now he runs Roadmap Labs and his own companies, Flexfolio and ReviewBuddy.
>
> *(Portrait: real photo of Dan)*
> I've been in business one way or another since I was 15, mowing lawns with Dad's mower. Then I did ten years in digital marketing. Social media led to copywriting, copy to design, design to websites and apps. Each step was a client asking for the next thing.
>
> The businesses I talk to aren't short on tools. Most pay a few thousand dollars a month for software and use a fraction of it. They know AI could help. They haven't had the time to work it out, their tools and data aren't set up for it, and they're not sure how to use it safely with client data. Most don't know where to start.
>
> Marketing goes the same way. They know they should do more of it, but there's no budget for an agency, no time to do it themselves and nobody they trust to hand it to. Meanwhile quotes, follow-ups and data entry eat the team's week.
>
> Roadmap Labs is how I fix that. We set up the tools and data first, then put AI to work one job at a time, and keep the marketing going out. I run my own companies, Flexfolio and ReviewBuddy, on the same systems. [TBC: refine after Dan's article idea]
>
> What we keep seeing
> **Why AI isn't working for most businesses yet.**
> 1 - **No time to work it out.** The team's flat out running the business. Nobody has a spare week to learn the tools and test what works.
> 2 - **Tools that don't connect.** A few thousand a month on software, most of it underused, with the data spread across all of it.
> 3 - **Unsure it's safe.** If you can't say where client data goes and who sees it, it's easier not to start.
> 4 - **Marketing keeps slipping.** No budget for an agency and nobody you trust with it. Admin takes the hours that marketing needed.
>
> How we work
> **The right job first, then the build.**
> 1 - **The right thing first.** Building is the easy part now. Working out what's worth building is where most of our time goes.
> 2 - **A month to prove it.** Every automation gets a one-month test. If it isn't working, we switch it off and try the next one.
> 3 - **Written for people.** Anything we publish for you has to be worth a real person's time. Writing for the algorithm doesn't count.
> 4 - **Foundations first.** Your tools and data get set up before the clever bits, so AI has something real to work from.
>
> Our own companies
> **We run our own companies on the same systems.**

> 02 Flexfolio · A portfolio platform for product managers. Our own company, in beta, on our systems. · View → `/projects/flexfolio`
