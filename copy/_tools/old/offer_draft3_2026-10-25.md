## 1. The offer in one line

**Rec.:** One monthly retainer for your software, automations and marketing automations. A$3,500 a month with AI running costs and GST included, and no contract.

**Alt:** We take the admin off your team and put your growth work on a system. One retainer, no contract.

## 2. What we do, who it's for, why it matters

**What we do.** We build:
- software around how your team works
- automations for the quotes, follow-ups, onboarding and data entry that eat the week
- marketing automations that keep your SEO, social posting and email going out, written for people first.

It's built one thing at a time and looked after by us, inside one monthly retainer.

**Who it's for (draft, from the audit Page).** Owner-led businesses of 5 to 10 or more staff:
- the tools are spread around and underused, often a few thousand dollars a month with no return
- there's no time, budget or trusted person for marketing
- admin eats the team's week, sometimes with a VA to brief
- they need to compete with bigger players without a bigger team.

*[DECISION: lead industry, from the audit options: property and finance services, or service businesses that quote.]*

**Why it matters.** Internal work like admin and quotes costs money every week. Growth work like SEO, social and email is the first thing dropped in a busy one. An SEO campaign, an ad agency and a VA add up to about A$5,170 a month, and most of it is never measured.

## 3. What Roadmap Labs builds

1 - **Software development.** Software built around how your team works, from a company portal to an internal app. Client builds sit inside the retainer. Compass OS, a private build for Compass Buyers Agency, is being built in Retool.
2 - **Automations.** Admin, quotes, follow-ups and onboarding, built one at a time and managed by us.
3 - **Marketing automations.** SEO, social posting and email that keep going out every week, written for people first.

We also build our own products. Flexfolio and ReviewBuddy are our own companies, and both run on the same systems.

## 4. How it works (full version)

**1 - Free discovery session.**
We sit down with you and go through where your team's time and money go: the jobs done by hand, the tools you pay for and barely use, and the growth work that keeps getting pushed to next week. Then we scope it and map out which automations are worth building, and in what order.

**2 - A roadmap with the numbers.**
Before you commit to anything, we work backwards from the result. For each initiative and automation, we estimate what it could save your team and the growth it could bring. Then we lay out a clear timeline: when each one gets built, when it should be running and when it should start paying back. You get it in writing, with the working shown. They're estimates, not promises. There's no pressure: look over the numbers, then decide.
- *[DECISION: turnaround]*

**3 - The retainer build.**
If the numbers stack up, we start the retainer at A$3,500 a month, with AI running costs and GST included.
- We build one automation at a time, as many as you need.
- Every automation gets a one-month test. If it isn't working, we switch it off and build the next one.
- When you're ready, we build your company portal. It's usually a month of work, at the same fee.
- We look after everything we build, pick the model for each job and watch what it costs. AI running costs are in the fee, up to a fair-use limit. *[DECISION: fair-use limit]*

## 5. Pricing

**A$3,500/mo incl. GST, with AI running costs included.** One retainer, the same at every stage. That's about A$808 a week (3,500 × 12 ÷ 52 = 807.69), or A$3,182 ex GST. The weekly figure is a supporting line only.
**What's not included:** other software you already pay for, like your CRM, which stays on your accounts. AI running costs are included, up to a fair-use limit. *[DECISION: fair-use limit, and whether automation platform and hosting fees count as running costs]*

### What it replaces

| What a business pays for | Per month [CONFIRM GST basis] |
|---|---|
| SEO campaign, well run | A$2,000 |
| Agency fees: Meta, Google or social ads | A$1,000 |
| Virtual assistant at A$500+ a week | A$2,170+ |
| **Together** | **About A$5,170** |
| **Roadmap Labs** | **A$3,500 incl. GST** |
| Difference | About A$1,670 |

Maths: VA A$500 × 52 ÷ 12 = A$2,166.67. Together A$5,166.67, minus A$3,500, is A$1,666.67, so about A$1,670. This **assumes the benchmarks include GST** (flagged in NOTES). If they're ex GST, compare A$3,182 ex GST, and the gap is about A$1,985.
Footnote: *Typical costs from Dan's ten years running an agency. The ad row is the management fee, not ad spend. Our fee includes AI running costs and GST.*
