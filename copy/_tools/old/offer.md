## 1. The offer in one line

**Rec.:** We build and manage automations for the work that keeps slipping. One flat fee of A$3,000 a month incl. GST, and no contract.

**Alt:** One monthly fee. Unlimited automations, built one at a time. Cancel any month and it's all yours.

## 2. What we do, who it's for, why it matters

**What we do.** We build automations for the jobs a business needs done but rarely gets to: SEO, social posting, email marketing, admin and sales processes. We build them one at a time, test every one and keep them running. When you're ready, we build a company portal that puts your data, systems and team in one secure place, so every automation after that has more to work with.

**Who it's for.** Businesses that know this work matters but can't justify a hire or an agency for each part of it. *[placeholder: Dan to confirm the buyer: industry, size and who signs off. This hasn't been answered yet.]*

**Why it matters.** This is the work that grows a business, and it's usually the first thing dropped in a busy week. Paying separately for SEO, ads and a VA adds up fast, and most of it is never measured.

## 3. What Roadmap Labs builds

1 - **Automations (the lead offer).** The monthly subscription below.
2 - **Internal operating systems.** A company portal for your data, systems and team. It's part of the subscription when a client is ready for it. Compass OS, for Compass Buyers Agency, is being built in Retool.
3 - **Our own products.** Flexfolio and ReviewBuddy are our own companies, and both run on Roadmap Labs systems.

## 4. How an engagement works

**Prove it, bring it together, grow it.**

1 - **Prove it.** We start with a couple of automations on the job that costs you most or never gets done. Every automation gets a one-month test. If it isn't working, we switch it off and build the next one. You don't spend three months and A$9,000 finding out something doesn't work.

2 - **Bring it together.** When the value is clear, we build your company portal. It puts your data, systems and team in one secure place, and the automations can work from it. It's usually a month of work, and the fee doesn't change.

3 - **Grow it.** We keep building, one automation at a time, as many as you need. We manage and secure every one, and keep each on the best AI model for the job at the best cost.

You don't have to start with the portal. Most businesses see more value sooner from a couple of working automations, so that's where we start.
