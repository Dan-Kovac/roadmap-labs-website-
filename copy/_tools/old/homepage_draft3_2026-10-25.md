## Hero options, ranked (no price)

The buyer: an owner-led business of 5 to 10+ staff. Admin is eating the team's week, tools are spread around and underused, and there's no time or trusted person for marketing (from the audit Page). Each option takes a different angle. The designer renders the top two. All four share the eyebrow, button, link and microcopy.

| Rank | Angle | Grey line (≤ 32) | Ink line (≤ 32) | Lead (≤ 150) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 (rec.)** | The buyer | Owner by day. Admin by night. (29) | Hand the admin to a system. (27) | For owner-led teams: software, automations and marketing automations that take the quotes and admin off you. It starts with a free discovery session. (149) |
| **2** | Cost | Paying for tools no one uses? (29) | Put your tools to work. (23) | We build software, automations and marketing automations that get your CRM and other tools doing the admin. It starts with a free discovery session. (148) |
| 3 | The team's time | Admin your team does by hand. (29) | Done by a system, every week. (29) | Software, automations and marketing automations that stop quotes, follow-ups and onboarding eating the week. Start with a free discovery session. (145) |
| 4 | Competing | The big players post every day. (31) | Now your business can too. (26) | Software, automations and marketing automations that keep your SEO, social and email going out, no extra hires. Start with a free discovery session. (148) |

**Why 1:**
- It names the buyer, the gap the audit flagged first.
- It describes the owner doing quotes after hours, which is a trigger the audit expects owners to recognise.
- The ink line is a plain outcome.

**Why 2:**
- It's a cost the owner already pays every month, and it speaks to tools that are spread around and underused.
- It's the strongest "cut internal costs" angle.

**Options 3 and 4:**
- 3 is the safest literal read.
- 4 is the growth and competing angle. It leans on marketing, so it undersells the admin side.

- **Eyebrow (≤ 40):** Melbourne · Software and automations (36). Alt, pairs with hero 1: For owner-led teams · Melbourne (31).
- **Button:** Book a call. **Text link:** How it works → (14).
- **Microcopy (≤ 60):** Free discovery session. See the numbers before you pay. (55). Alt A: Start free. See the numbers before you pay a cent. (50). Alt B: First step: a free session to map what's worth automating (57).
